Shipment PO-4471 of Product C from Gulf Coast Components was scheduled to arrive at the Florida distribution center on September 3, 2026.
The shipment arrived on September 14, 2026, 11 days late, because of a port closure at the supplier's origin port.
Product C was out of stock at the Florida distribution center from September 6 to September 14, 2026.
