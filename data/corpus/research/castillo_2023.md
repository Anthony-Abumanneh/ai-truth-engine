In a preregistered randomized placebo-controlled trial of 8,000 adults, stroke occurred in 0.2% of the zantorel group and 0.4% of the placebo group over three years.
This is a relative risk reduction of 50% but an absolute risk reduction of 0.2 percentage points, so about 500 people must take zantorel for three years to prevent one stroke.
The trial was funded by the manufacturer of zantorel.
