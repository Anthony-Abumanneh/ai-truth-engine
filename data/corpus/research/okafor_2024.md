In a preregistered multicentre randomized controlled trial of 612 adults, veridamine did not significantly improve sleep quality compared with placebo (difference 2%, p = 0.41).
The trial was publicly funded. The authors conclude that the earlier pilot finding of an 18% improvement was not replicated.
