In a prospective cohort of 24,000 adults followed for 12 years, people who drank brivo tea daily had a 20% lower risk of dementia than non-drinkers.
Brivo tea drinkers also had higher education and income, and the authors note that residual confounding cannot be ruled out because the study was observational.
No randomized trial of brivo tea and dementia has been conducted.
