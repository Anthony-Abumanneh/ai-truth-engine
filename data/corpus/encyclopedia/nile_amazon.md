The Nile is usually listed as the longest river in the world at about 6,650 kilometres.
However, the title is disputed: some studies that measure the Amazon from a more distant source in Peru put the Amazon at about 6,992 kilometres, which would make the Amazon the longest river.
The result depends on how the source and the mouth of each river are defined, so there is no single agreed answer.
