The Eiffel Tower is a wrought-iron lattice tower on the Champ de Mars in Paris, France. It was completed in 1889 as the entrance arch to the 1889 World's Fair.
The tower was built by the engineering company of Gustave Eiffel. The initial design was produced by the engineers Maurice Koechlin and Emile Nouguier.
The Eiffel Tower is 330 metres tall including its antennas. Without antennas the structure was originally 300 metres tall.
It was the tallest man-made structure in the world until the Chrysler Building in New York City was completed in 1930.
