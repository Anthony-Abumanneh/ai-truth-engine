The theory of relativity comprises special relativity and general relativity, both developed by Albert Einstein.
Relativity influenced modern physics profoundly, reshaping cosmology, astrophysics and particle physics.
Modern physics, including relativity, contributed to the Global Positioning System: GPS satellite clocks must be corrected for relativistic effects of about 38 microseconds per day, or positioning errors would accumulate at roughly 10 kilometres per day.
