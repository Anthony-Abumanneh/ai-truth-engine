Albert Einstein was a theoretical physicist born on 14 March 1879 in Ulm, in the Kingdom of Wurttemberg in the German Empire.
Einstein developed the special theory of relativity in 1905 and the general theory of relativity in 1915.
Einstein received the 1921 Nobel Prize in Physics for his services to theoretical physics and especially for his discovery of the law of the photoelectric effect. The Nobel Prize was not awarded for the theory of relativity.
Einstein emigrated to the United States in 1933 and died on 18 April 1955 in Princeton, New Jersey.
