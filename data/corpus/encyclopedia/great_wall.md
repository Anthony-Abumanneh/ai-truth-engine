The Great Wall of China is a series of fortifications built across the historical northern borders of ancient Chinese states.
A 2012 survey by China's State Administration of Cultural Heritage measured the total length of all sections of the wall at 21,196 kilometres.
The Great Wall is not visible to the naked eye from the Moon. Astronauts report that it is generally not visible to the naked eye from low Earth orbit either, because the wall is narrow and similar in colour to the surrounding terrain.
